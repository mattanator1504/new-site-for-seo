## How to use these numbers in content

- **Specific, never rounded up.** "$7.2M in pipeline", not "over $7 million". "142 demos", not "nearly 150".
- **Always say where a number came from.** "across client campaigns", "for one SaaS client", "on one campaign". Never imply every client gets the featured result.
- **No outcome promises.** Show what happened, never what will happen. "This client went from $2M to $7M" — yes. "You'll triple revenue" — never.
- **No borrowed industry stats** unless sourced and linked in the post. If you can't link it, cut it.
