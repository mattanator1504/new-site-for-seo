## The $2M → $7M SaaS story

A B2B cyber security SaaS company was stuck at roughly $2M ARR. Matthew redesigned their enterprise buyer funnel, sharpened their cold email positioning, and built an automated demo booking system. Nine months later: $7.2M ARR, 142 enterprise demos booked, 3.8x faster pipeline.

**The bones to keep:** it wasn't one magic tactic. It was three pieces fixed together — the site, the outbound message and the booking step. That's the Full Growth System argument in one story.

**Specific detail to use:** the 142 demos. It's a real, odd number. Odd numbers feel true.

**Use when:** writing about the Full Growth System, outbound for SaaS, enterprise sales funnels, or why fixing one piece rarely fixes the whole pipeline.

**Never:** name the client, or imply this is a typical result.

## How to use these

- **Don't recite them.** Adapt the bones — the business type, the one specific thing that was fixed, the one number — to fit the post.
- **One story per post, max.** More feels like a portfolio page.
- **Always include one concrete detail** — a number, a buyer type, a tool that was built.
- **Use client names only where the site already names them publicly**, and only for the project description. Never attach a number to a named client unless it's in their public case study.
- **Never invent a new story or a new number.** If nothing fits, skip the story.
