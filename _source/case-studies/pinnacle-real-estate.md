## The short version

Pinnacle Real Estate Group works with buyers and sellers across Los Angeles County. They wanted a pipeline that didn’t depend only on referrals and ads. We ran a targeted campaign across key LA County neighborhoods and vetted every lead before handoff. In two months: 30+ hot leads, several of which closed.

## The situation

Real estate teams live and die by the pipeline. Pinnacle had referrals and some advertising, but wanted a source they could scale and count on month to month.

They also didn’t want volume for its own sake. Agents’ time is the expensive part, so every lead had to be worth a call.

## What was built

### Local market targeting

Prospective buyers and sellers across key LA County neighborhoods.

### Lead vetting

Every lead qualified before handoff, so the team only spent time on serious prospects.

### Steady delivery

A consistent outreach cadence over the full period, so the pipeline stayed full instead of arriving in one burst.

## What happened

Over 2 months, the campaign delivered 30+ vetted, hot leads to Pinnacle’s team.

Several of those leads converted into closed transactions, giving the team a lead source that sits alongside their referral network.

## What I learned

Vetting is the unglamorous part, and it’s the part agents notice. Ten serious leads beat fifty “just browsing” ones every time.
