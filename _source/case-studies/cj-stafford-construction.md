## The short version

CJ Stafford Construction relied on word of mouth and wanted a pipeline it could predict. We ran a focused 6-week outbound campaign aimed at decision-makers who need construction work, and rebuilt the website so it backed up every email. Phase 1 added 25 new leads.

## The situation

Referrals are lovely until they stop. CJ Stafford needed new project leads that didn’t depend on who happened to mention them this month.

There was a second problem: outbound sends people to your website, and the old one didn’t show the company’s work or credibility well enough to back up a cold email.

## What was built

### Narrow targeting

Decision-makers likely to need construction services, with commercial property managers as a key audience. A specific buyer with a specific job, not anyone with a building.

### A 6-week Phase 1

A focused outreach cycle to test messaging and see how the market responded before scaling.

### A new website

Built to show CJ Stafford’s work, credibility and services, so prospects who clicked through found a reason to reply.

## What happened

In six weeks, Phase 1 added 25 new leads to CJ Stafford’s pipeline.

The rebuilt site now supports every outbound touchpoint, which sets up later phases to scale.

## What I learned

Outbound and a credible website work better together. A great email that lands on a weak site is like a firm handshake followed by a mumble.
