## The short version

Sync'd was a passion project: a digital hub where South Africa’s emerging artists, writers and podcasters could show their work and meet each other. As co-founder and product lead, I shaped everything from the editorial direction to the UX. When the app idea proved too heavy, we moved to a web-first platform built on three pillars: Read, Listen, Connect.

## The situation

There was no shortage of talent. There was a shortage of attention. Established names dominated galleries, airtime and search results, and new voices struggled to find an audience or collaborators.

## What was built

### Read

A magazine-style layout that puts artwork front and center, without distractions.

### Listen

A podcast page with one-click playback and short interviews tagged by discipline and location.

### Connect

A networking event flow to RSVP, swap details and chat in small themed breakout rooms that opened 15 minutes before each event.

### Curated spotlight

A rotating Artist of the Week banner that brought in applications and page views for new talent.

## What happened

Early feedback from creative Slack groups and social comments pointed to two needs: visibility (work front and center, not buried in a feed) and community (low-pressure ways to meet peers). The platform was built around both.

## What I learned

A platform’s power isn’t in having more features. It’s in solving the right problems simply. Three pillars beat thirty features.
