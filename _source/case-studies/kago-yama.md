## The short version

Kago Yama is a farming app concept for smallholder farmers around Limpopo: manage plots, spot crop problems early and sell directly to buyers. I designed about 25 screens in Figma, from onboarding to a mini marketplace. It stayed a prototype, until a real farm adopted the wireframes in 2025.

## The situation

Smallholder farmers in Limpopo face unpredictable crop health with no easy way to spot pests early, operations tracked on paper, and buyers who are often hours away with no way to book digitally.

## What was built

### Farm profile

Sign-up with plot details, soil type and contact info.

### Daily tasks

A checklist tracking planting, spraying and harvest.

### Crop scanner

Upload a photo to flag common pests or diseases, shown as a rule-based demo.

### Marketplace and community

Buy supplies, list produce for local buyers, and share tips in a community feed that rewards activity.

## What happened

Every flow was built in Figma with clickable hotspots and sample data from regional crop guides, then refined over three review rounds. The goal: a farmer gets from “plot to sale” in under five taps.

In 2025, a real Kago Yama farm in Limpopo used the wireframes as a blueprint for its own digital record-keeping.

## What I learned

Mapping every flow end to end, before any code, showed which features actually mattered: photo diagnostics, clear task reminders and a simple produce listing. The rest was nice to have.
