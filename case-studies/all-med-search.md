## The short version

All Med Search places healthcare professionals in US hospitals. They needed more candidates and more facility clients without leaning on ads or job boards. We ran two cold email campaigns, one to Canadian nurses open to moving and one to US healthcare decision-makers. Together they booked 65 meetings.

## The situation

Recruiting has two sales problems at once. You need people to place, and you need places that want them. All Med Search wanted both: experienced nurses in Canada willing to relocate to the US, and US healthcare facilities that needed staff.

They wanted to do it through outbound, not by paying for more job board listings or advertising.

## What was built

### Two lists, two audiences

Experienced Canadian nurses open to relocation, and decision-makers at US hospitals and medical centers.

### Human, non-salesy sequences

Short emails written for each audience’s actual worries, not a generic recruiting pitch.

### A/B testing

Subject lines, body copy and calls to action tested to improve replies and protect deliverability.

### Follow-up sequences

Several follow-up touches to turn first replies into booked meetings.

## What happened

The nurse campaign sent 30,000 emails over months 1 and 2 and produced 45 meeting-ready leads.

The facility campaign sent 30,000 emails over months 1 to 3 and produced 20 meetings with healthcare decision-makers. That’s 65 meetings across the two campaigns.

## What I learned

The lesson I keep coming back to: one campaign can’t talk to two audiences. Nurses and hospital directors care about completely different things, so they got completely different emails.
