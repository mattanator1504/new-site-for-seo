## The short version

MegaLit was a utility app for Johannesburg residents tired of inaccurate water meter readings and painful bill payments. As lead UX/UI designer, I designed real-time meter readings, simple in-app payments and usage analytics, built to work for people with any level of tech confidence. It didn’t reach full launch.

## The situation

Residents dealt with inaccurate and delayed water meter readings, which led to billing disputes, and a payment process clunky enough to put people off paying on time.

Any fix also had to work for people with limited tech experience, not just the confident app users.

## What was built

### Real-time readings

Up-to-date meter readings to replace delayed manual ones.

### Simple bill payment

View and pay bills inside the app through a secure payment gateway.

### Usage analytics

Consumption over time, so residents could see and manage their usage.

### Inclusive by design

An accessible interface for every level of digital skill, available as both a mobile app and a web app.

## What happened

The work covered user research, task flows, information architecture, wireframes and final designs. The app didn’t reach full launch.

## What I learned

Designing for people who don’t trust the system yet means showing your working. A number on a bill is a dispute. A number with a reading history behind it is an answer.
