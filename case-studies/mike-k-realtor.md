## The short version

Mike K left pharmacy to sell real estate. New agent, no track record, competitive market. We built a brand-forward website that turned his background into a reason to trust him, and ran outbound to buyers and sellers in his area. It produced about 60 hot leads and multiple closed deals.

## The situation

A new agent has a chicken-and-egg problem. You need deals to look credible, and you need to look credible to get deals.

Mike needed three things at once: a steady flow of qualified buyer and seller leads, credibility without a long track record, and a professional site that reflected his brand.

## What was built

### A brand-forward website

A clean, professional site built around Mike’s story, listings and value, with a home valuation tool, because the best lead magnet answers the question the seller is already asking.

### Positioning his background

A pharmacist is detail-oriented and trusted with important decisions. We made that the reason to call him, not something to hide.

### Targeted outbound

Outreach to prospective buyers and sellers in his market.

## What happened

The campaign generated about 60 hot leads.

Multiple deals closed from that pipeline, and the site now works as Mike’s digital storefront.

## What I learned

A career change isn’t a weakness if you tell it well. Most new agents try to look like everyone else. Mike looked like Mike, and it helped.
