Consulting website design and SEO for a new fractional CEO firm

Fractional CEO offers executive expertise on demand. It was a new business with no website, in a competitive market. We built the site from scratch to explain the offer clearly and look the part, set up an SEO and content plan for the fractional executive niche, and put analytics in from day one.

01

The situation

New consulting firms have a credibility gap. Buyers are hiring senior judgement, so the website has to feel senior before anyone books a call.

Fractional CEO needed a site that explained a fairly new idea in plain language, and a way to be found by the people searching for it.

02

What we built

  1. 01A website built from scratchA responsive site presenting the mission, services and what makes the firm different, designed for professionalism and authority.
  2. 02Niche keyword researchAn SEO strategy built around how people search for fractional executive leadership.
  3. 03A content planArticles and success stories that show thought leadership, so the site keeps earning traffic after launch.
  4. 04Analytics at launchTracking for traffic sources, engagement and conversions from the first day live.
03

What happened

In the first few months after launch, traffic grew, a good share of visitors came from organic search, and the first inquiries arrived. The case study reports this without exact figures, so we’ve left them out.

When the offer is new, the website’s first job is explaining it. Fancy can wait. If a visitor can’t say what you do after ten seconds, they won’t book a call to find out.
