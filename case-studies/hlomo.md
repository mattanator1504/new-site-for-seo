## The short version

Hlomo was a concept to rethink emergency response in South Africa using trusted community networks. Over seven months at Sammi Studio, I co-founded it and handled strategy, UX/UI design and front-end development. The idea: one tap alerts neighbors and professional responders at the same time, through a calm, simple interface.

## The situation

Calling for help shouldn’t need a manual. The existing call center system is hard to navigate in a high-stress moment.

Vulnerable groups, including older people, refugees and people with disabilities, often struggle with jargon or poor network coverage. And people need to trust that an SOS will actually reach someone, fast.

## What was built

### A single-tap SOS

A bold button that works offline and confirms clearly once the alert is sent.

### An emergency circle

Pre-selected trusted contacts and organizations, set up before you ever need them.

### Dual notifications

Neighbors and responders alerted together, with clear icons and color cues.

### Cash and cashless options

So anyone can respond, whatever payment method they use.

## What happened

The concept came from secondary research and empathy mapping, then went into Figma designs. It was never tested with real users, and I’m upfront about that.

## What I learned

When someone is panicking, fewer taps and clear feedback beat any clever feature. Offline fallbacks and retry cues aren’t extras. A calm palette and short words matter more than you’d think.
