Construction company outbound: 25 new leads in 6 weeks

CJ Stafford Construction ran on word of mouth and wanted a pipeline it could predict. We ran a focused 6-week outbound campaign aimed at decision-makers who need construction work, and rebuilt the website to back up every email. Phase 1 added 25 new leads.

The situation

Referrals are lovely until they stop. CJ Stafford needed new project leads that didn’t depend on who happened to mention them that month.

There was a second problem. Outbound sends people to your website, and the old one didn’t show the company’s work or credibility well enough to back up a cold email.

02

What we built

  1. 01Narrow targetingDecision-makers likely to need construction services, with commercial property managers as a key audience. A specific buyer with a specific job, not anyone with a building.
  2. 02A 6-week Phase 1A focused outreach cycle to test messaging and see how the market responded before scaling.
  3. 03A new websiteBuilt to show CJ Stafford’s work, credibility and services, so prospects who clicked through found a reason to reply.
03

What happened

In six weeks, Phase 1 added 25 new leads to CJ Stafford’s pipeline.

The rebuilt site now supports every outbound touchpoint, which sets up later phases to scale.

Outbound and a credible website work better together. A great email that lands on a weak site is like a firm handshake followed by a mumble.
