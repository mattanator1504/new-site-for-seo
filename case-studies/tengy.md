## The short version

Tengy set out to fix the worst part of big events: the food and drink queue. As the UX/UI designer, I designed an app that lets attendees order from their seats and pay cashless, plus a real-time dashboard for vendors to manage orders. My role grew into product design and strategy along the way.

## The situation

At concerts and festivals, queues for food and drinks eat into the event people paid for, and vendors lose sales to the people who give up waiting.

The challenge was a smooth ordering flow for attendees and a dashboard vendors could run in a loud, busy, chaotic environment, without ignoring the technical and business constraints.

## What was built

### Remote ordering

Attendees order straight from their seats, so nobody has to choose between the headline act and a burger.

### Cashless payments

A secure, quick payment flow inside the app.

### A real-time vendor dashboard

Order management for vendors, built to cut wait times and keep service quality up during the rush.

### A design system

Shared components and task flows so the attendee app and vendor tools stay consistent as the product grows.

## What happened

The work covered task flows, system architecture, initial designs and the design system for the attendee app and vendor dashboard.

## What I learned

Designing for an event is designing for distraction. People are tipsy, the music’s loud and the battery is at 9%. Every extra tap is a sale lost to the queue you were trying to remove.
